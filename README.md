♻️ EcoScan
Your Smart Sustainable Waste Management & Recycling Platform
EcoScan is an AI-powered waste management platform designed to help users identify, categorize, 
and responsibly dispose of everyday household waste and electronic garbage in one place. 
From quick image scanning and proper disposal guidelines to scheduling local e-waste pickups and earning eco-rewards, 
EcoScan brings fragmented waste management efforts into a single, organized dashboard. 
The goal is simple: less landfill waste, better recycling compliance, and a cleaner environment.
🚀 Why EcoScan?
Modern households and businesses face major challenges in practicing responsible waste disposal:
•	♻️ Uncertainty about recyclable vs. non-recyclable materials
•	🔌 Difficulty finding authorized e-waste collection centers
•	📦 Accumulation of unused or broken household items
•	📅 Lack of scheduled pickup services for bulk recyclable waste
•	🌿 Low motivation or incentives for daily waste segregation
•	📍 Fragmented local government or private recycling information
These issues usually lead to improper waste disposal, environmental pollution, and valuable recyclable materials ending up in landfills.
EcoScan brings everything together into one intelligent waste management hub.
🛈 Problem Statement
People struggle with daily waste management across multiple disconnected offline and online channels.
A local recycling schedule might be on a paper flyer, e-waste guidelines scattered online, and private collection centers operating without centralized listing.
This fragmentation increases the chances of:
•	🚫 Mixed waste dumping and improper disposal
•	🛑 Accumulating hazardous e-waste at home
•	⏱️ Spending unnecessary time searching for pickup services
•	💸 Missing out on green incentives and eco-rewards
•	📉 Low overall community participation in sustainability drives
EcoScan aims to solve this problem by creating a unified AI-driven waste management ecosystem.
💡 Our Vision
EcoScan aims to become an essential daily utility for sustainable living — a single platform where users can instantly identify waste materials, learn how to dispose of them properly, and easily access local recycling infrastructure.
🛠️ Key Features
📱 AI Image Scanner
Instantly scan household items using a smartphone camera to classify waste into bio-degradable, recyclable, non-recyclable, or e-waste categories. 
📍 Local Center & Pickup Finder
Locate nearby authorized waste recycling centers, scrap dealers, and municipal collection points using interactive maps. 
📦 E-Waste & Bulk Pickup Scheduling
Book doorstep pickup slots for bulk electronic waste, old appliances, and recyclable items directly through the platform. 
🎁 Gamified Eco-Points & Rewards
Earn eco-credits for every verified scan, successful waste donation, or recycling pickup, redeemable for discount vouchers or green perks.
📘 Smart Disposal Guidelines
Access step-by-step instructions on cleaning, dismantling, or safely handling hazardous materials before disposal.
📊 Personal Environmental Impact Tracker
Monitor personal carbon footprint reduction, total weight recycled, and positive environmental impact over time.
